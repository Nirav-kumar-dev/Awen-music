description:: Awen 1.0.5 brings real-time YouTube Cloud watch history and playback synchronization, cloud history browsing, obsidian crystal branding, and an animated dancing mascot on the mini player.

### What's New
- **Real-Time YouTube Cloud Playback Sync**: Songs played in the app are automatically synchronized with YouTube's servers in real-time under your logged-in Google account, feeding YouTube's recommendation algorithm with accurate watchtime and playback signals.
- **YouTube Cloud History Browsing**: Toggle between Local History and YouTube Cloud Watch History in the History screen with a dedicated refresh button.
- **Obsidian Black Crystal Branding**: Sleek new obsidian crystal app icon and brand emblem.
- **Dancing Mascot on Mini Player**: Animated mascot that dances during music playback, bounces on entrance, paces smoothly across the player, and shows an upset reaction when paused.

### Bug Fixes & Improvements
- **Modern Google Cookie & SAPISID Authentication**: Full support for `__Secure-3PAPISID`, `__Secure-1PAPISID`, and `SAPISID` with dynamic `SAPISIDHASH` generation.
- **Seamless Local Fallback**: When logged out of YouTube, the app continues to operate 100% locally with zero disruption or network overhead.
- **Performance & Background Sync**: Optimized watchtime heartbeat reporting with low memory and battery usage.
